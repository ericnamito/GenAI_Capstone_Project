# GenAI_Capstone_Project
Repository containing all the necessary files for the final Capstone Project as part of the GenAI for Humanists SoSe 2026 course.

## Brief intro 
Democratic participation depends not only on the right to access legislation, but on the genuine ability to understand it. In Romania, as in many post-communist democracies, legislative texts are characterized by dense juridical language, bureaucratic syntax, and layers of cross-referencing that render them effectively inaccessible to the majority of citizens. This project proposes the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) to transform Romanian government bills and policies into plain, more accessible language. The goal is not merely technical translation, but a humanistic intervention aimed at closing the civic literacy gap and empowering ordinary citizens to engage with the laws that govern their lives.
